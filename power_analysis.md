# Power Analysis Notes

## Internal power

Power dissipated within cells during internal switching activity.

## Switching power

Power associated primarily with charging/discharging net capacitances.

## Leakage power

Static power consumed by cells even without logic transitions.

## Project result

Total power = 7.00554 mW

Breakdown:
- Internal = 4.17627 mW
- Switching = 2.71907 mW
- Leakage = 0.11020 mW
